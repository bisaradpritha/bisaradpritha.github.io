The Journey

PhD Chemistry/Computational Biology, ASU · Research Scientist, Phoenix Children's & UA College of Medicine - Phoenix · 6 peer-reviewed publications

01 / 04

Origin

My interest in natural sciences began with a simple query: how do complex systems work at molecular level? That question led me to chemistry, where I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees while building a strong foundation in molecular science and analytical thinking. Looking back, chemistry wasn't my final destination—it was the framework that taught me how to break complex real-world problems into fundamental pieces that are connected mechanistically.

02 / 04

Exploration

My PhD broadened both the scientific questions I pursued and the tools I used to answer them. While studying monoclonal antibody–peptide interactions, I worked at the intersection of chemistry, molecular biology, computational biology, and machine learning. This interdisciplinary environment reinforced the importance of integrating experimental and computational approaches to solve complex biological problems and sparked my interest in using computation to uncover patterns that are difficult to detect through experimental methods alone.

03 / 04

Current Mission

My current research focuses on rare neurological movement disorders, integrating genomics, bioinformatics, and neural signal analysis to investigate disease mechanisms and identify opportunities for improved diagnosis and treatment. Transitioning from peptide chemistry to human genetics and neuroscience has provided excellent opportunities to continually build expertise across multiple disciplines while learning and applying new computational approaches to clinically relevant research questions.

04 / 04

Outside the Lab

Curiosity doesn't end when I leave the lab. I enjoy painting, crafting, reading, and exploring new technologies simply because I like understanding how things work. Building this website has become another creative outlet—an opportunity to combine programming, design, and storytelling into a project that reflects the same mindset I bring to research: learn continuously, build thoughtfully, and never stop experimenting.

That's the journey. What follows is the record—pitstops, waypoints, milestones, and a few stopovers along the way. Tap into whichever one you're curious about.
