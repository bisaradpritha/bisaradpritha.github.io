The Journey

01 / 04

Origin

My interest in natural sciences started with a simple query: how do complex systems work at molecular level? That question led me to chemistry, where I completed both my bachelor's and master's degrees while building a strong foundation in molecular science and analytical thinking. Looking back, chemistry wasn't my final destination—it was the framework that taught me how to break complex real-world problems into fundamental pieces connected mechanistically.

02 / 04

Exploration

My PhD expanded both the scale of the questions I could ask and the tools available to answer them. Working at the intersection of chemistry, molecular biology, computational biology, and machine learning showed me that meaningful discoveries often happen between traditional disciplines. I became increasingly interested in using computation not simply to analyze biological data, but to reveal patterns that would otherwise remain hidden.

03 / 04

Current Mission

My current research focuses on rare neurological movement disorders, integrating genomics, bioinformatics, and neural signal analysis to investigate disease mechanisms and identify opportunities for improved diagnosis and treatment. Transitioning from peptide chemistry to human genetics and neuroscience has allowed me to expand my expertise across multiple disciplines while applying computational approaches to clinically relevant problems.

04 / 04

Outside the Lab

Curiosity doesn't end when I leave the lab. I enjoy painting, crafting, reading, and exploring new technologies simply because I like understanding how things work. Building this website has become another creative outlet—an opportunity to combine programming, design, and storytelling into a project that reflects the same mindset I bring to research: learn continuously, build thoughtfully, and never stop experimenting.
